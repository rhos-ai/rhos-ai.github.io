About RHOS

Building physical AI systems for robots in the real world.

RHOS brings together world modeling, embodied data, motion control, and robotic hardware to build robot foundation systems that improve through real physical interaction.

Company

From embodied data to deployed robot behavior.

We believe embodied intelligence is a central path toward general intelligence because it connects perception, action, contact, failure, and feedback in one continuous learning loop.

Our work focuses on the full stack required for physical AI: collecting high-quality interaction data, training physical world models, deploying policies on real robots, and using execution outcomes to improve the next generation of models.

Team

RHOS is assembling a team across model algorithms, data systems, motion control, and robotic hardware. The shared goal is simple: make learning systems that can survive the complexity of real physical environments.
